How food sharing works

From Enquiry to Distribution

  1. Donation enquiry

    A donor contacts us.

  2. Assessment

    We check food suitability and quantity.

  3. Collection

    We coordinate pick-up.

  4. Safe handling

    Food is stored and transported safely.

  5. Distribution

    Food reaches intended recipients.

Food safety first. All food handling and distribution must follow applicable food safety and hygiene requirements. Food that is unsuitable will not be distributed.

What This Can Achieve
